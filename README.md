# 42 Lyon - Robotic Club Exercises

This repo contains multiple exercises to learn embedded electronics and programming for beginners, intermediate and people interest by the embedded engineering domain. 

Those Exercises are meant to be done by the members of our Robotic Club from our campus, but you can use them to train your skills. 

## Available Chapter

The actual Chapter list open in this repository

**Chapter List:**
- Chapter 1: MCU Basics

## How to do the exercises

Those exercises are meant to be relatively general and can be made with any MCU at your disposition. 

For the members of the Robotic Club, you can ask for the Main memebers to borrow one of the Arduino pack to make those exercises. Try to build some group of 2 or 3 person to enforce the peer to peer learning and better Club material dispatch. 

A lot of exercises will demand you to have some basic materials (Leds, breadboard, jumper cable, button... etc), look at the recommanded material in the Chapter exercises file and try to organize yourself to bring it (ask someone for the material, make some group command to limit the prices...etc)

## Evaluation 
Evaluation system are destined to the members of the Robotic Club.

There is no Evaluation rating or system implemented for the moment in the Club for the moment but it will soon be updated to make a interactive system favoriting the peer to peer learning within our community. Check the Club discord for any update.
